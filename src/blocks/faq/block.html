How long does it take to move a studio into Sable?

Most teams import their projects and clients from a CSV in an afternoon. We map the columns with you and keep the original file, so nothing is lost if you change your mind.

Do clients need an account to review work?

No. Clients open a private link to read briefs, approve milestones and pay invoices. Add a password to any link when the work is confidential.

Can we invoice in several currencies?

Yes. Each client has a default currency and every invoice can override it. Reports convert totals at the day's rate, so revenue stays comparable month to month.

What happens when we add or remove seats?

Seats are prorated to the day. Removing someone keeps their work, comments and time entries in the studio; they simply stop being billed.

Where is our data stored, and can we take it with us?

Workspaces are hosted in the EU and encrypted at rest and in transit. You can export every project, invoice and file as CSV or ZIP at any time, from any plan.

Can we cancel at any time?

Yes, from the billing page, without a call. Your workspace stays readable for 30 days afterwards so you can export what you need.
